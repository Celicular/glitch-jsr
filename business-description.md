About Glitch
Play • Relax • Repeat

Glitch is a social gaming lounge built for casual gaming sessions, friendly competition and time spent with friends. Visitors can choose from PlayStation gaming setups, a PC driving experience with a steering wheel, and refreshments available at the lounge.

The website should convey an energetic, welcoming atmosphere and help visitors quickly understand what Glitch offers, how much it costs and where to find it.
